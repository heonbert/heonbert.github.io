# Visitors

Six figures (grandfather, grandmother, mother, father, girl, boy) that walk through the hall in three small families.

They were generated from text prompts with Tripo AI (text to 3D, retopology to about 20,000 triangles, automatic rigging on a Mixamo skeleton, preset "walk" and "idle" animations), then compressed with glTF Transform (1024 px WebP textures, Meshopt).
They are not portraits of real people.
