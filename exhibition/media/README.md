# Media

- `simpo-2019.mp4`: 92 seconds cut from seven clips the photographer filmed at Simpo Port, Gimje, on 24 January 2019 (Canon EOS 5D Mark IV). Silent, 960 x 540.
- `simpo-2019.mp3`: a 57-second loop of the sound his camera recorded with those clips, filtered to reduce wind noise.
- `simpo-2019.jpg`: still frame shown before the film starts.
